Privacy Policy

QuantumClock is a standalone clock application designed to provide a visual, quantum-inspired timekeeping experience.

Information We Collect

QuantumClock does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information or user data.

QuantumClock does not require an account and does not request access to contacts, location, photos, health information, or other personal data.

Tracking

QuantumClock does not track users or use advertising or tracking technologies.

Third-Party Services

QuantumClock does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Data Security

Because QuantumClock does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no user personal data stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of QuantumClock change in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.
